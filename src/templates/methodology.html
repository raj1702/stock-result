Methodology & data sources

ResultLens converts reported financial trends into an auditable Earnings Quality Score. It describes the consistency and alignment of recent results; it does not predict a share price or issue a buy/sell recommendation.

Methodology last updated: 6 September 2026

Data used

The analysis uses the five most recent reported quarters available. A future or unreported quarter is never treated as a zero.

  • Primary financial source: NSE corporate financial filings and their reported XBRL facts.
  • Fallback financial source: Upstox Fundamentals when comparable NSE filing history cannot be obtained.
  • Quote and valuation fallbacks: selected values such as P/E or market capitalisation may use Yahoo Finance when NSE leaves the field blank.
  • Unavailable observations remain blank. The result screen identifies the financial source used for that company.

Loan books are often disclosed half-yearly. Blank quarters are skipped and consecutive reported observations are compared instead.

How points are earned

Consistency components

Each consecutive pair of reported observations is evaluated: an improvement earns full credit, an unchanged value earns half credit, and a decline earns no credit. The component receives that proportion of its weight.

Margin components

Half of the component weight reflects whether the latest margin is positive. The other half reflects how consistently the margin improved across reported periods.

Missing information

A component without enough observations is marked unavailable rather than scored as zero.

Final score = earned points ÷ available component weight × 100

Losses and PAT quality

A narrowing loss counts as an improvement for PAT consistency, while the screen still states that the company remains loss-making. PAT points can be reduced when PAT rises without revenue support and expanding margins appear to be the main driver, because that pattern may be less durable.

Automated explanation

The AI Smart Signal is an automated narrative built from the same displayed metrics. It checks whether PAT is supported by revenue or NII, whether margins explain a gap, and whether operating profit or the loan book supports the topline trend. The component calculations remain visible so users can audit the conclusion.

Limitations and responsible use

ResultLens is an analytical aid, not investment advice. Filing data can be delayed, restated, incomplete or affected by exceptional items and accounting changes. A five-quarter trend cannot capture valuation, balance-sheet risk, asset quality, management quality, industry conditions or every cash-flow consideration. Verify figures against the original exchange filing and consider qualified professional advice before making an investment decision.
